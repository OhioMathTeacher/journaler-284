Journaler — a quick start

Your writing home for the term · TCE 284 · everything stays on your computer

Journaler is where you do your writing this term. It runs in your browser, and nothing you write is uploaded — it lives on your computer. There are four tabs across the top: Freewrite, Currere, Readings, and Notebook.

The four tabs

Freewrite start here

The five One-Pagers live here. Each one runs in two steps:

  1. Gush — set the timer and write. While it runs, Backspace and Delete are off. You cannot fix anything, so keep going. Stalled? Write that you stalled.
  2. Shape — when the buzzer sounds, a composer opens beside your gush. Build your one page from the raw material.
Currere

The four moments — Regressive, Progressive, Analytical, Synthetical. Same gush-then-shape rhythm, with the structure loosening as you go.

Readings

Load a chapter with ＋ Load readings (pick several at once) or ＋ Load a folder. Switch chapters from the dropdown. Read one page at a time, or flip to Continuous.

Notebook

Your kept pages, seen two ways: By day (a calendar) or By piece (one piece across the whole term, so you can watch it change).

Keeping a page

Your drafts save themselves as you type. When a page is worth keeping, press ＋ Add to notebook. That dates it and files it in your Notebook.

Come back to it. Open the same piece weeks later, write more, and add to notebook again. Both passes stay, dated, so your thinking shows over time.

Saving & moving your work

  • ⤓ Save my work (top bar) downloads one file with everything you have written.
  • Put that file wherever you keep things — Google Drive, Dropbox, or a thumb drive.
  • On any computer, open Journaler and use ⤒ Open my file to pick up where you left off.
Do this often. Your work is saved in this browser, but a lab computer can be wiped. The saved file is your real backup.

The AI partner (optional)

Connect a model with the AI button, top right. After a gush, a reflection partner asks how the writing felt — it never touches your words. Journaler works fully without AI, too.
