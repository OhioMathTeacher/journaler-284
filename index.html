About Journaler-284

A place to write, read, and keep a journal across the term. Built for TCE 284 at Miami University.

Where your work lives

On this computer, in this browser. Nothing you write is uploaded, and there is no account and no server. Your writing saves itself as you type, so it is still here when you come back. But it lives in this browser: clearing your browser data erases it, and a lab computer can be wiped without warning.

Can I keep it on Google Drive or Dropbox?

Yes, and you should. ⤓ Save my work downloads a single file holding everything you have written. Put it on Drive, Dropbox, or a thumb drive, and open it later with ⤒ Open my file — on this computer or any other. Your freewrites, One-Pagers, currere, notebook, highlights, and reading conversations all come back.

The one thing that file does not carry is the readings themselves, since those are yours rather than the app's. After you restore, point the Readings tab at your chapter folder again with ＋ Load a folder — loaded that way, your highlights and notes reattach to the right chapter.

Save often. That file is your real backup, and it is the only copy that is not sitting in one browser on one machine.

When you connect an AI

The AI is optional and off by default. With a local model, nothing leaves this computer. With a hosted model, what you ask about — the passage, your question, and the page you are reading — is sent to that provider to answer it.

About our icon

A lotus above an open book. The lotus roots in mud and blooms anyway, which is the shape of this course: you go back to what you did not choose, and you write forward from it. The book is where that happens.
