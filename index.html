Your work

Saved in this browser as you type. Clearing browser data erases it, and lab machines get wiped — so use ⤓ Save my work, which downloads one file holding everything.

Drive or Dropbox?

Yes. Keep that file anywhere and reopen it with ⤒ Open my file, on any computer. Your writing, notebook and highlights all come back. Your readings do not — reload the same files with ＋ Load readings and your highlights reattach to them.
